# iTunes Library Keeper
{: .fs-9 }

Keeps **iTunes for Windows** in step with the music folders on your disk.
Scan, review, then apply.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

[Download](https://github.com/faljanubi/itunes-library-keeper/releases/latest){: .btn .btn-primary .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 .mr-2 }
[Install guide](installation){: .btn .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 }

![Overview page in light and dark mode]({{ '/screenshots/overview.png' | relative_url }})

iTunes doesn't notice when you add, move, rename or retag music files outside it. Your library
fills up with "missing file" entries, new albums never show up, and edited tags stay stale.

iTunes Library Keeper finds all of that for you:

- **New files** in your music folders that aren't in iTunes yet
- **Moved or renamed files**, relinked in place so ratings, play counts and playlists are kept
- **Missing files** whose track can't be found anywhere
- **Edited tags** that iTunes hasn't re-read yet
- **Unavailable drives**, reported separately and never treated as missing

Nothing in iTunes changes until you've reviewed the list and clicked **Apply**. If you want,
**automation** can then keep everything in step in the background.

It can also build a **folder tree of playlists** from your tags, such as
*Albums by Artist › B › The Beatles › Abbey Road (1969)*, and keep it up to date.
